Bhoomi (“the Earth”) never cared about Earth. Now her life depends on it.

2150. A solar superstorm kills every chip on the Moon in eleven minutes. Everyone who could help is on Mars. To survive long enough to call Earth, Bhoomi has to learn how Earth got here: the fixes, the failures, and the people. She finds all of it in the junk and the records they left behind.

The sequence

A prologue, six chapters and an epilogue, all rendered from content.js, so this page and the game can't drift apart. Every historical event is real and sourced; the fiction is Bhoomi, her family, the storm and the rescue. The route: Shackleton → Malapert A (the library) → Mons Mouton (the drill) → Vikram (the mirror).

Decided

  • Eras: true to each era's camera. Engraving → 16mm B&W → colour film → VHS (1980–99) → early digital → HD.
  • Build: all 6 chapters for Saturday.
  • Indian thread: one Indian achievement or sacrifice in every chapter, always after the danger has passed.
  • Her roots: revealed by the last plaque. Her great-grandmother Chandrika Devi (“moonlight”, fictional) was on India's first crew to live at Shackleton.
  • Nambi Narayanan: one factual line, inside the cryogenic-engine story.
  • Ending: she goes to Earth to study, at PRL Ahmedabad, Sarabhai's lab, and keeps the Mars dream. “Mars isn't cancelled. It's rescheduled.”

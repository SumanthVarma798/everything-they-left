## The story (v4; full script in `content.js`, rendered by `story.html`)

**2150. Bhoomi (“the Earth”), a Lunarian born on the Moon, who has ignored Earth her whole life.**
A solar superstorm kills every chip on the Moon in eleven minutes. Everyone who could help is on Mars.
To survive long enough to call Earth, she has to learn how Earth got here, from the plaques she never
read, the Lunar Library on IM-1 Odysseus (30 million pages on nickel, real, near Malapert A) and the
junk and failures humanity left behind. The 56 failed missions become her parts catalogue.

Fiction: Bhoomi, the storm, the rescue. Real: every mission, date, object, distance and number.

### Two rules
1. **Danger first, wonder after.** DANGER (2150 HUD) → SEARCH (her pencil notebook) → ACT (interaction)
   → BREATH (safe for now) → WONDER (she relives the people behind it, in colour).
2. **Each time has its own camera** (`lib/era.js` + `lib/era.css`): the present is pencil; the relived
   past plays through its era's camera: ≤1899 engraving · ≤1965 16mm B&W · ≤1979 colour film ·
   ≤1999 VHS · ≤2012 early digital (orange date) · 2013+ HD/livestream. (VHS for everything
   pre-2000 is a one-line change if chosen.)

## Specs (all built and tested in `lib/`; try them in `prototypes.html`)

### Timer / checkpoint / switch (lib/interactions.js + lib/hud.js)
- Timer = SVG rect with `pathLength=100` draining via CSS animation; `animationend` = fail.
- **The SVG viewBox must match the panel's pixel size** (ResizeObserver). A fixed 100×100 viewBox
  with `preserveAspectRatio=none` makes the side strokes about 5× thicker. (lib/interactions.js)
- Fail = red **edge** pulse (shields-down), not full-screen; small alert top-centre; replay the
  interaction (checkpoint). The 3rd miss shows the "turn off the timer ↗" toast once.
- Vertical Apple-style switch top-right: on = green, knob up, ⏱; off = red, knob down, ⏱ struck through.

### Visor HUD (lib/hud.js → `mountHud`)
Objective top-left · segmented O₂ bar + compass with salvage waypoints top-centre · clock + timer
switch top-right · salvage tracker (radar sweep) bottom-left · power/water bottom-right · visor
curvature + scanlines at the rim only · prompts like `DRAG ITEMS` / `TAP DOT · HOLD DASH` inside the
interaction panel, lower third. Boot animation per element, driven by `hudUnlock` in content.js.

### Pencil notebook (lib/notebook.js → `ensurePencil`, `drawPlan`)
- `#pencil` SVG filter: greyscale → colour-dodge with its blurred negative → gamma → levels →
  hatch grain → **tinted onto paper inside the filter** (rotated "taped" photos block `mix-blend-mode`).
- **color-dodge order:** `in` = blurred negative, `in2` = grey. Swapped gives an embossed look.
- Works best on people and hardware; dark lunar landscapes get busy, so keep those as photos.
- His plans: rough.js (`vendor/rough.js`, 4.6.6) paths that draw themselves via `strokeDashoffset`;
  handwriting in Caveat.

### Interactions (lib/interactions.js → `mountInteraction`, configured by `interaction` in content.js)
- **drag**: pointer drag and also click-item-then-click-target (keyboard / single-pointer, WCAG 2.5.7).
- **balance**: native range inputs; available power falls; win when sum ≤ available and every load ≥ its min.
- **morse**: pointer/Space hold on the reflector; ≤250 ms = dot, ≥400 ms = dash, anything in
  between is ignored with a shake; a wrong symbol resets; dot/dash buttons as a fallback.
- **hold-breath**: pointer/Space hold, counts in tenths; under 1 s → "Take a real breath."

### Salvage map (lib/archive-map.js → `mountSalvageMap`: paper page, then “Look up ▸” lifts into lib/attempt-map.js)
139 Moon-bound missions from `data/missions.json`, rough.js arcs Earth→Moon: red failed (✕),
amber partial, graphite success, ochre crewed (dashed return). Named sites at **real near-side
coordinates** (orthographic); salvage pins for the A11 mirror, A12 nuclear battery, IM-2 drill,
Vikram mirror; "me (Bhoomi)" at Shackleton. Year slider + Play (lingers 2.6 s on beat years).
Tally counts up to "139 tries · 56 failed".

### 3D stage (lib/stage3d.js → `createStage`)
three.js r170 from `vendor/` (works offline). Moon `SphereGeometry(1,192,192)`, 4k map,
bumpMap = colour map; star sphere; Earth over the near side; sun + **earthshine** light (lights
the ground in the night Signal shot). `flyTo(wp, s)` arcs out and back; `snap(wp)` for direct links.
Waypoints from `crises[].waypoint`; the camera stays off the pole axis to avoid lookAt flips.
**Kill switch:** no WebGL → static Earthset photo (verified: headless Chromium has no WebGL
and falls back cleanly). Known ceiling: the equirect texture pinches at the pole; upgrade to
LROC polar maps when NASA SVS is back.
